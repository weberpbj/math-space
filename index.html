English

This policy describes how the Math Space app (Google Play ID com.weberpbj.math.space) handles data. The app is made for children who use it together with a parent or guardian.

In short: the app sends no data about the child off the device. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account on our server — there is no server.

Who is the controller

Branimir Jakirčević, Zadar, Croatia. Contact for any privacy question: weberp.bj@gmail.com.

What is stored, and where

Everything listed below stays on the device the app is installed on. It is not sent to our server (we do not operate one), not shared with third parties and never used for advertising.

What the app never collects

The child’s full name, date of birth, e-mail address, photos, voice, location, contacts, messages, files, advertising identifiers, health data or any usage analytics. The app has no sign-in and no user account.

Google services inside the app

Purchases run through a Google service, for which Google acts as an independent controller:

  • Subscription and one-time purchase (Google Play Billing). Payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled by Google Play. The app never sees or stores card details; it only learns whether a subscription is active or the one-time purchase was made.

This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is built for families. The child never registers and never types in personal data. A profile is only a name or nickname entered by the parent, kept on the device. Purchases, the parent overview and every link that leads out of the game sit behind the parental PIN. The app shows no ads.

Deleting data

  • In the app: For parents → Privacy and data → Delete all data. This removes every profile, all progress, the daily challenge and the PIN.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.
  • That does not cancel the subscription, which is managed by Google Play: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions. The one-time purchase stays with the Google account it was paid from.
  • For questions or a data request write to weberp.bj@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

How long data is kept

As long as the app is installed, or until you delete it as described above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing. Since the data stays on your device you control it directly; for anything else use the contact address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country or with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, Zagreb.
