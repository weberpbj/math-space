Ova pravila opisuju kako aplikacija Math Space (Google Play identifikator hr.mathspace.app) postupa s podacima. Aplikacija je namijenjena djeci koja je koriste uz roditelja ili skrbnika.

Ukratko: aplikacija ne šalje nikakve podatke o djetetu izvan uređaja. Nema oglasa, nema analitike, nema praćenja i nema korisničkog računa na našem poslužitelju — poslužitelja nema.

Tko je voditelj obrade

Što aplikacija ne prikuplja

Ime i prezime djeteta, fotografije, glas, lokaciju, kontakte, poruke, datoteke, identifikatore za oglašavanje, podatke o zdravlju ni bilo kakvu statistiku korištenja.

Google usluge u aplikaciji

Dvije radnje odvijaju se kroz Googleove usluge i za njih je Google samostalni voditelj obrade:

  • Prijava Google računom (Credential Manager / Sign in with Google). Roditelj bira račun; aplikacija od Googlea dobiva adresu e-pošte i zapisuje je na uređaj. Prijava se koristi samo za potvrdu odrasle osobe i za vraćanje PIN-a.
  • Pretplata (Google Play Billing). Plaćanje, obnovu, otkazivanje i povrat vodi Google Play. Aplikacija ne vidi i ne pohranjuje podatke o kartici; dobiva samo podatak je li pretplata aktivna.

Na te obrade primjenjuju se Googleova pravila privatnosti.

Djeca

Aplikacija je izrađena za obitelji. Dijete se nigdje ne registrira i ne unosi osobne podatke. Profil je samo ime ili nadimak koji roditelj upiše i koje ostaje na uređaju. Kupnja, postavke i svaka poveznica izvan igre zaštićeni su roditeljskim PIN-om. Aplikacija ne prikazuje oglase.

Brisanje podataka

  • U aplikaciji: Za roditelja → Privatnost i podaci → Obriši sve podatke. Time se brišu svi profili, napredak, PIN i zapis o prijavi.
  • Deinstalacijom aplikacije brišu se svi njezini podaci s uređaja.
  • Pretplata se time ne otkazuje jer je vodi Google Play: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
  • Za pitanja ili zahtjev u vezi s podacima pišite na weberp.bj@gmail.com; odgovaramo u roku od 30 dana.

Koliko se podaci čuvaju

Dok je aplikacija instalirana ili dok ih ne obrišete gore opisanim postupkom.

English

This policy describes how the Math Space app (Google Play ID hr.mathspace.app) handles data. The app is made for children who use it together with a parent or guardian.

In short: the app sends no data about the child off the device. There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no account on our server — there is no server.

Who is the controller

Branimir Jakirčević, Zadar, Croatia. Contact for any privacy question: weberp.bj@gmail.com.

What is stored, and where

Everything listed below stays in the storage of the device the app is installed on. It is not sent to our server (we do not operate one), not shared with third parties and never used for advertising.

Child profile name (a nickname typed in by the parent)telling profiles apart in the gamedevice

Backups are switched off: app data is not copied into Google backup or device transfer.

What the app never collects

The child’s full name, photos, voice, location, contacts, messages, files, advertising identifiers, health data or any usage analytics.

Google services inside the app

  • Sign in with Google (Credential Manager). The parent picks an account; the app receives the e-mail address from Google and stores it on the device. It is used only to confirm an adult and to restore the PIN.
  • Subscription (Google Play Billing). Payment, renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled by Google Play. The app never sees or stores card details; it only learns whether a subscription is active.

For those operations Google acts as an independent controller under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is built for families. The child never registers and never types in personal data. A profile is only a name or nickname entered by the parent, kept on the device. Purchases, settings and every link that leads out of the game sit behind the parental PIN. The app shows no ads.

Deleting data

  • In the app: For parents → Privacy and data → Delete all data. This removes every profile, all progress, the PIN and the sign-in record.
  • Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from the device.
  • That does not cancel the subscription, which is managed by Google Play: play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.
  • For questions or a data request write to weberp.bj@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days.

How long data is kept

As long as the app is installed, or until you delete it as described above.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure and restriction of processing. Since the data stays on your device you control it directly; for anything else use the contact address above. You may also lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, Zagreb.
